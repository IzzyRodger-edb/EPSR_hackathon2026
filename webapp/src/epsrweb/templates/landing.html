Refine a structure against your diffraction data, and get the figures into your paper

Upload the Gudrun .mint01 files for one material. The agent reads them, proposes a model system, generates the EPSR inputs and runs the Rust EPSR engine. You get the fit, the partial pair distribution functions, the refined empirical potential, a PDF report, and a button that commits any figure straight into the figures/ folder of your Overleaf project.

Create an account Sign in What do I need?

1. Upload

Two to four isotopic samples of the same material, as Gudrun .mint01 tables. The files are checked before anything is queued: three numeric columns, ascending Q, within EPSR's limits.

2. Refine

A scratch start, an equilibration on the reference potential, then refinement with the empirical potential switched on. You watch the iterations, the R-factor and the engine log as they happen.

3. Publish

Tick the figures you want. They are pushed to Overleaf over its Git bridge as PDF, with a ready-made \includegraphics block for each. No Overleaf subscription? Download the same thing as a zip.
