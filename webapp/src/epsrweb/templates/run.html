How to read these numbers
R-factor
Mean squared difference between data and simulation over the fitted Q range. For a liquid against good neutron data it falls to a few times 10-4 (water reaches 2.3 × 10-4). A crystal modelled with intermolecular bonds only stays near 10-2.
R squared
Near 1 means the simulation follows the shape of the data.
EP amplitude at ereq
The empirical potential is as large as it is allowed to be. A larger ereq may improve the fit, but a large EP means the reference potential is poor.
Pressure
Should be modest, a few kbar, for a liquid at its measured density.
Coordination number
Neighbours within the first minimum of g(r). For water, about 5 oxygens within 3.45 A and 1.8 hydrogen-bonded hydrogens per oxygen.
